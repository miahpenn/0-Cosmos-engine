## 1. Do not merge the three identities

The ledger has three separate lanes:

1. The continuum covariant identity for the total stress tensor.
2. The actual semi-discrete matter RHS: signed face-flux differences plus the implementation's geometric and exchange sources.
3. The spherical Misner–Sharp mass identity on a declared worldtube.

They use different dependent quantities and different hypotheses. A cellwise Valencia RHS decomposition is not an independent proof of \(\nabla_\mu T^\mu{}_\nu=0\); a frozen-state total-\(T\) divergence is not an evolving-state proof; and a Misner–Sharp mass-rate remainder is not the momentum-constraint propagation residual from Issue #4.

## 2. Continuum total-stress identity and moving-shell form

Adopt the source convention

\[
\nabla_\mu T^\mu{}_\nu = Q_\nu ,
\]

where \(Q_\nu=0\) only when the relevant *total* tensor has no unassigned exchange/source. In the mixed-index convention used by the engine, the identity is

\[
\partial_\mu\!\left(\sqrt{-g}\,T^\mu{}_\nu\right)
=\sqrt{-g}\left(\frac12 T^{ab}\partial_\nu g_{ab}+Q_\nu\right).
\]

In spherical symmetry the 1-D code omits the angular \(\sin\theta\) factor; the full spherical integral carries an overall \(4\pi\). With the zero-shift scope of the current shell observer, define

\[
E_t=\sqrt{-g}\,T^t{}_t,\qquad
F_t=\sqrt{-g}\,T^r{}_t,\qquad
W_t=\frac12\sqrt{-g}\,T^{ab}\partial_t g_{ab}.
\]

Then

\[
\partial_t E_t+\partial_r F_t=W_t+\sqrt{-g}\,Q_t.
\]

For a shell bounded by moving coordinate locations \(r_i(t)\), with coordinate speeds \(v_i=\mathrm{d}r_i/\mathrm{d}t\), define the outward-positive *relative* face flux

\[
F_{\mathrm{rel},t}(r_i)=4\pi\,[F_t-v_i E_t]_{r_i}.
\]

The shell residual is therefore

\[
\mathcal R_t =
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dt}\left(4\pi\int_{r_i(t)}^{r_o(t)} E_t\,\mathrm dr\right)
+F_{\mathrm{rel},t}(r_o)-F_{\mathrm{rel},t}(r_i)
-4\pi\int_{r_i(t)}^{r_o(t)} W_t\,\mathrm dr
=4\pi\int_{r_i(t)}^{r_o(t)}\sqrt{-g}\,Q_t\,\mathrm dr .
\]

This equality defines the balance residual and its sign; it does **not** assume the right-hand side vanishes. If the shell boundaries are fixed in coordinate radius, \(v_i=v_o=0\). If they are fixed in areal radius \(R_*\), they generally move through coordinate space:

\[
v_R=\left.\frac{\mathrm dr}{\mathrm dt}\right|_{R_*}
=-\frac{\left.\partial_t R\right|_r}{\partial_r R}.
\]

The boundary term must use \(F_t-v_R E_t\), not \(F_t\) alone. The shell observer reconstructs the metric work and boundary motion from the same base / plus / minus tangent states. Its current-source witness is frozen, zero shift, and advances no production step; it does not test nonzero-shift closure or the wave-generator/time-flow hypothesis.

The radial-covector equation is separately

\[
\partial_t(\sqrt{-g}\,T^t{}_r)
+\partial_r(\sqrt{-g}\,T^r{}_r)
=\sqrt{-g}\left(\frac12 T^{ab}\partial_r g_{ab}+Q_r\right).
\]

It contains the radial metric-gradient contraction and cannot be replaced by the time-shell ledger.

## 3. The semi-discrete Valencia budget is a different statement

The generic mixed-variable contract in [engine/valencia.py](https://github.com/miahpenn/0-Cosmos-engine/blob/4aec88d0e3ff028e0a65a8ac36b30948625df792/engine/valencia.py) is

\[
U_D=\sqrt{-g}\,J^t,\qquad
U_t=\sqrt{-g}\,T^t{}_t,\qquad
U_r=\sqrt{-g}\,T^t{}_r .
\]

For the species RHS used by the engine, write each channel schematically as

\[
\dot U_{s,i}
=-\frac{\widehat F_{s,i+1/2}-\widehat F_{s,i-1/2}}{\Delta r}
+S^{\mathrm{geom}}_{s,i}
+Q^{\mathrm{exchange}}_{s,i}
+Q^{\mathrm{rest}}_{s,i}.
\]

This is a description of the actual discrete assembly, not a license to infer a missing physical coefficient. In the pinned code:

- The face fluxes are generated by the production reconstruction/HLL path and include the spherical metric/area factors in the reduced radial representation; the full angular integral adds \(4\pi\). Face orientation is increasing coordinate radius, so the finite-volume divergence is \(-(\widehat F_{\rm out}-\widehat F_{\rm in})/\Delta r\).
- The inner face is assigned zero by the current spherical-centre implementation; the outer face is separately evaluated/extrapolated. Do not silently discard either boundary contribution in a global ledger.
- The energy and momentum channels separately retain the geometric metric sources and the conditional exchange projections. The variable-mass rest-current source \(q_D\) is its own channel; it is not the same term as the energy source \(q_E\).
- Radiation uses a separate Eulerian conservative representation, \(U_E=\sqrt{\gamma}E\) and \(U_r=\sqrt{\gamma}S_r\), in the production radiation branch. Do not claim that the generic mixed-\(T^\mu{}_\nu\) expressions above literally describe that branch without its separate flux/source conversion.

For a fixed uniform grid, summing the semi-discrete equation gives the exact telescoping form

\[
\Delta r\sum_i\dot U_{s,i}
=-(\widehat F_{s,\mathrm{out}}-\widehat F_{s,\mathrm{in}})
+\Delta r\sum_i S_{s,i},
\]

where \(S_{s,i}\) denotes the sum of the explicitly retained source channels for that variable. This is a discrete RHS identity. It does not prove that the sum of sector stress tensors has zero covariant divergence or that their normalizations match the gravitational constraint convention.

## 5. Misner–Sharp balance: fixed coordinate and fixed areal radius are not interchangeable

The pinned spherical geometry uses

\[
R=\frac{r\sqrt b}{X},\qquad
K^\theta{}_\theta=\frac K3-\frac{A_a}{2},
\]

\[
\chi=\gamma^{rr}(\partial_rR)^2-(R K^\theta{}_\theta)^2,\qquad
m_{\rm MS}=\frac R2(1-\chi)
\]

for the zero-shift witness. The worldtube helper computes these geometric observables, but its ordinary summary chooses the cell nearest a **coordinate** radius; it is not the fixed-areal-radius ledger.

For zero shift, the same-stage areal-radius tangent is

\[
\left.\partial_tR\right|_r
=R\left(\frac{\dot b}{2b}-\frac{\dot X}{X}\right).
\]

The coordinate-fixed spherical mass identity recorded in the archive is

\[
\left.\partial_tm_{\rm MS}\right|_r
=4\pi R^2\left[-\alpha\gamma^{rr}j_r\,\partial_rR
-p_r\left.\partial_tR\right|_r\right]
\quad (G=1).
\]

Define the signed current/work pieces

\[
F_{\rm MS}=-4\pi R_*^2\alpha_*\gamma^{rr}_*j_{r,*}(\partial_rR)_*,
\qquad
W_p=-4\pi R_*^2p_{r,*}(\partial_tR)_*.
\]

For a tube of fixed areal radius \(R_*\), the tube moves in coordinate space. Hence

\[
\left.\frac{\mathrm dm_{\rm MS}}{\mathrm dt}\right|_{R_*}
=\left.\partial_tm_{\rm MS}\right|_r
+v_R\,\partial_rm_{\rm MS},\qquad
v_R=-\frac{\partial_tR}{\partial_rR}.
\]

The predicted fixed-\(R\) rate is \(F_{\rm MS}+W_p+v_R\partial_rm_{\rm MS}\). The ledger remainder is the **signed** measured fixed-\(R\) mass rate minus this prediction. It must preserve both evaluation orders:

- **Factorized/native:** interpolate the primitive factors at \(R_*\), multiply them there, and retain the native spatial mass-gradient/tangent operator.
- **Composite:** form each local flux/work product first, interpolate that product, and use the piecewise-linear mass-versus-\(R\) slope for the moving-tube sweep.

The two are distinct discrete operators. The current signed remainders differ in sign at some resolutions; choosing the smaller residual would be an unauthorized physical closure patch.

The mass-rate formula is not the spatial mass-gradient identity. The latter, with the pinned constraint convention, is

\[
\partial_rm_{\rm MS}
=4\pi R^2\left(\rho_{\rm total}\,\partial_rR
+j_r\frac{\partial_tR}{\alpha}\right)
+\frac{R^2\partial_rR}{4}\,\mathcal H
+\frac{R^2}{2}\frac{\partial_tR}{\alpha}\,\mathcal M_r
+\epsilon_{\rm op},
\]

\[
\mathcal H=H_{\rm geom}-16\pi\rho_{\rm total},\qquad
\mathcal M_r=M_{\rm geom}-8\pi j_r.
\]

Here \(\epsilon_{\rm op}\) is a retained discrete-operator remainder, not a physical source to fit. Its measured value is sensitive to derivative and interpolation order. The Hamiltonian term, momentum term, operator term, and fixed-\(R\) mass-rate remainder must stay separate. In particular, the Hamiltonian contribution to a sampled mass gradient is not an extra term to subtract from the independently computed fixed-\(R\) rate residual.

The archived bridge record's coordinate-fixed formula and its later areal-coordinate special case are consistent in scope only after the index/sign convention is aligned: in an areal coordinate, \(R=r\), \(\partial_tR=0\), and the pressure-work term vanishes. Do not transfer that special-case formula unchanged to the engine's general CMC radial coordinate.
