SEA_ANSWER said the sea's answer "opens" at x_A > 16 H/mu, "at production's H_today/mu = 0.0057 only x_A > 0.09".
That is the condition for the instantaneous growth rate to beat dilution. It is not the condition for the drain to matter.

Corrected (Derived; toy-checked in `results/dr-return-paths-2026-11-15/`): a D wave crossing the resonance gains
exp(pi mu^2 / H) in number, mu = (3/32) x_A. Order-one gain needs x_A > 6.0 sqrt(H/mu) = 0.45 at production;
draining S's ringing from a seed of relative size sigma needs x_A > 6.0 sqrt(H/mu) sqrt(ln 1/sigma^2) = 3.07 for
sigma = 1e-10, above the hold band. At production H/mu the drain is seed-limited; the seed is an unpaid input.
