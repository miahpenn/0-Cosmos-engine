# #595 against Aster's material (comparison only, no run)

Source: Aster's README-34 (5b206037), centre report (fed74597) and reproduction ZIP, on Drive in 0-Cosmos-engine/research (ZIP SHA256SUMS verified). Script `research/w1_fring_a6/aster_compare.py`, numbers in `aster_compare.json`.

## What explains the gaps
1. **(A) peak |M| at dr 0.0125: the input.** Aster's dr 0.0125 slice (SHA-256 0cc47fb1...) and ours differ by at most 1.6e-16 in S, PS, D, PD (1e-13 relative); the two Newton solves were seeded differently (Aster: the dr 0.025 member; ours: the dr 0.05 A1 harmonics) and stop at residuals 6.9e-12 and 4.0e-12. At t 0 the readbacks agree (cell-0 M 1.370661e-16 vs 1.370726e-16, same peak H and M). By the first sample (t 0.00375) cell-0 M differs by about 10% and stays 10-30% apart to t 0.015. At dr 0.025, where the inputs are identical, every sample matches to all printed digits. So M at about 1e-11 on the fine grid carries the imprint of roundoff-level input differences; a 1e8 amplification in ten steps is more than the field equations should give, and one inferred route is the implicit solves converging to a tolerance (not checked).
2. **Band ratios: mostly the band definition.** Aster's band is the max over 17 areal radii R 0.05-0.25 with cubic interpolation (R_areal = r sqrt(b)/X); ours was the max over cell centres. Recomputed on our runs with Aster's definition (R taken as r times the first-cell areal scale, 1.000085): dr 0.025 reproduces Aster's 2.3333e-12 and 1.3087e-12; the ratio dr 0.0125/0.025 becomes 1.58x and 2.35x (Aster 2.01x and 3.57x; ours on cell centres 3.87x and 4.02x). The rest of the gap is item 1.
3. **(B) centre fix: a different design (plus item 1).** Aster's trial writes only a, b and Aa at cell 0 (Aa/r^2 and (a/b-1)/r^2 extrapolated from cells 1-3, a b^2 held fixed), inside the production stage hooks (explicit predictor, primary predictor, final corrector, each before the lapse solve). It leaves B, beta and Lambda alone, does no determinant normalization and keeps the current D_r Aa. Ours replaced D_r Aa by regularized_aa_prime in three vendor functions and wrote a, b, Aa, beta, Lambda and B (= 0.75 Lambda) at cell 0 after each accepted step. The 8x vs 14x cell-0 fall and the band ratios are not comparable across the two designs.

A match to fed74597 can now be checked only by running Aster's scripts or input, which is a new run.
