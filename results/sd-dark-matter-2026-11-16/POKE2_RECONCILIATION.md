# POKE-2: reconciling projects' |H|/m_S ≈ 0.30 with chat's ≈ 0.002–0.006 (Nov 16 2026)

> **Naming (Nov 17):** "m_S" in this note is ω_S in the sea, 2μ (μ = S's bare mass, the unit). See
> [`AMENDMENT-1-mass-naming.md`](AMENDMENT-1-mass-naming.md).

Chat's toy estimate put |H|/m_S at the hold-band crossing near today's value (H_today/m_S = 0.0057/2 = 0.0028), on the
reasoning that under the one-budget rule today's dark-matter density already sits near the band. Projects' lane gives
0.30 (README §4). The two use the same conventions, so this is not a normalisation error:

- **S's mass:** both take m_S = 2 in the sea, in units where S's bare mass is 1 and H_today = 0.0057.
- **x from the lane's density:** x = λ ρ_E / (2κ) for a small ring (scaled energy e = λ ρ_phys = m_S² ⟨s²⟩ = 2x). At
  production's λ this gives x_today = 0.807, below the band edge 4/3. So today is not past the band on the number
  ruler. The mean-field energy ruler would put today's density past the band-edge energy, which is why §4 carries two
  rulers.
- **The difference is |H| at the crossing.** The crossing happens on the way back down, after turnaround, and the
  contraction is not the time-reverse of the expansion. It is driven by φ's falling potential and is kinetic-dominated.
  From production's lane (f_D = 0.76):

  | contracting through | t from today | H | \|H\|/m_S |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | a = a_today | 5.901 Hubble times | −64 H_today | 0.18 |
  | a = 0.846 a_today (x = 4/3, number ruler) | 5.903 | −106 H_today | 0.30 |

  At the same scale factor as today, the contracting universe's |H| is 64 times today's. Estimating |H| at the
  crossing as today's value assumes a symmetric bounce-like history the lane doesn't have.

**Filed:** projects' 0.30 stands, as a lane-level number on the production ruler λ. Chat's estimate is superseded on
this point. The verdict "S does not cleanly ring at the crossing" stays ruler-dependent through λ (README §4), and the
lane carries S–D matter as dust, which is itself outside its validity where |H| ~ m_S.
